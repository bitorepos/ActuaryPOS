# SEO Phase 3: industry compatibility

Scope: industry compatibility requested for the Programmatic Surface Phase 3 brief, checked against this RetailMan ERP repository on 2026-09-09. No production pages, database records, licensing terms or ERP behavior were changed.

The existing untracked `SEO_PHASE_3_ARCHITECTURE.md` was present before this task. Its rental and domain assumptions are superseded by the findings below; its proposed CREATE decisions are not evidence that pages exist. It has been preserved.

## Critical corrections

1. **Hms means Hotel Management System in this repository.** `modules/Hms/module.json:4` explicitly describes room bookings, extras and coupons. `HmsBookingController::store` creates hotel booking transactions, room lines, arrival/departure dates and extras. The separate `Clinic` module implements outpatient appointments and queues. Do not publish a Hospital Management page based on Hms. The [company homepage](https://programmaticsurface.com/) currently labels it as a hospital system; confirm whether a different hospital product exists before correcting that catalog record or commissioning hospital copy.
2. **Rental is implemented here.** The earlier draft's statement that no dedicated rental module was verified is outdated. `RentalAgreementController::store` creates agreements, rental lines, period-based charges, deposits and stock reservations; `RentalReturnController::store` records returns and deposit refunds. This supports a rental workflow description, but does not prove overbooking prevention, live vehicle tracking or production readiness.
3. **Industry suitability is configuration-dependent.** Optional module files being present does not mean every plan includes them, every module is activated, or every external device/API is compatible. Describe the specific workflow and required module; confirm deployment and licensing separately.
4. **Keep the domains distinct.** Provider-led industry implementation and quotation pages belong on ProgrammaticSurface.com under this brief. RetailManERP.com remains the product/documentation source. Do not implement the company architecture blindly in this product CMS.

## Publication order and acceptance gate

Start with an industries hub and a small first group: retail/wholesale, manufacturing, repair and warehouse; retain the existing restaurant URLs. A later group can cover rental, gym, tailoring, imports and logistics after the modules are demonstrated on the supported release. Clinic, fuel and eCommerce copy need their explicit constraints checked. Hotels require the catalog correction; hospitals remain on hold.

For each detail page, require a unique introductory answer, business workflow, required modules, configured deployment choices, relevant documentation, real related-page links, a quotation CTA, breadcrumb and useful questions. Do not fill absent sections with invented integrations. Keep warehouse physical stock, import costs and fleet dispatch as distinct topics.

Before publishing: verify licensed release/module activation, demonstrate the described workflow, confirm exact license/support language, reconcile existing company CMS pages, check one H1 and unique title/description, validate self-canonical/schema, HTTP 200 and sitemap inclusion, and ensure no broken links. No medical, tax, financial or legal compliance claim is approved by this source audit.
