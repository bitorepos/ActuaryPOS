# Domain positioning for Phase 3

This strategy follows the supplied Programmatic Surface brief. It does not change routing, canonicals or branding in this workspace.

| Domain | Primary responsibility | Content treatment |
|---|---|---|
| RetailManERP.com | Product capabilities, usage guides, product documentation and subscription evaluation | Explain what the product does with verified workflows. Keep existing guide URLs and product positioning. |
| ProgrammaticSurface.com | Provider-led industry configuration, module purchase, deployment/implementation and quotation | Explain business requirements, required modules, implementation scope and contact/quotation process. Use exact verified contract terms for licensing. |

Provider industry pages may name RetailMan ERP naturally, but should not reuse identical feature copy or compete with its technical guides. Link to the product site for detailed instructions and subscription registration. Link back to the company only where a user needs a purchase, implementation or deployment service.

Maintain self-canonicals for genuinely distinct pages on each domain. Do not cross-canonicalize unique company pages to the product homepage. Reconcile duplicated existing content before deciding on merge/redirect actions; preserve indexed URLs when their intent remains useful.

The company already exposes `/restaurant-pos-software`, `/restaurant-erp-software` and `/inventory-management-software`. Review and reuse those URLs before creating alternatives. `/products` remains the purchase/pricing destination; a future `/industries` or `/solutions` hub should organize requirements rather than duplicate its catalog.

The earlier workspace architecture draft proposes product-domain feature and industry CREATE actions. Those decisions do not authorize copying this company brief into RetailMan's CMS. See `SEO_PHASE_3_INDUSTRY_COMPATIBILITY.md` for the corrected industry scope and evidence.

License duration, domain/device limits, source-code rights, updates, support and synchronization guarantees need authoritative business terms and release evidence. Website marketing text alone is not proof of those rights or capabilities.
