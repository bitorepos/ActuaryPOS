## 1. Introduction

### What Is the HRM & Essentials Module?

The **HRM & Essentials** module adds powerful human resource management and day‑to‑day business tools to your POS system. It is split into two sections:

| Section | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| **HRM** | Manages employees, attendance, shifts, leave, payroll, holidays, warnings, awards, announcements, employee documents, leave quotas, and sales targets. |
| **Essentials** | Provides tasks/to-do lists, company documents & memos, personal reminders, internal chat/messaging, and a knowledge base. |

Both sections work together to help you run the "people" side of your business without needing a separate HR system.

### Who Should Read This Manual?

- **Business owners / managers** who need to set up and oversee HR operations.
- **HR administrators** who manage attendance, leave, payroll, and employee records.
- **Regular employees** who clock in/out, apply for leave, view payslips, and use tasks/messaging.

## 4. Departments & Designations

### 4.1 Departments

**What is it?** Departments let you organize employees into groups (e.g., Sales, Accounting, Warehouse, Kitchen).

**How to get here:** HRM → **Departments** tab.

**What you can do:**

1. **Add a Department:**
   - Click **+ Add** button.
   - Enter the **Department Name** (e.g., "Sales").
   - Optionally enter a **Department Code** (e.g., "SALES-01").
   - Click **Save**.

2. **Add a Sub-Department:**
   - When adding a department, select a **Parent Department** to make it a sub-department.
   - Example: "Online Sales" as a sub-department under "Sales".

3. **Edit a Department:**
   - Click the **Edit** (pencil) icon next to the department name.
   - Change the name or code and click **Update**.

4. **Delete a Department:**
   - Click the **Delete** (trash) icon.
   - Confirm the deletion.

> **Important:** Departments created here are used throughout the HRM module — in payroll, leave, attendance reports, and announcements.

### 4.2 Designations

**What is it?** Designations are job titles (e.g., Manager, Cashier, Supervisor, Accountant).

**How to get here:** HRM → **Designations** tab.

**What you can do:**

- **Add a Designation:** Click **+ Add**, enter the designation name, and click **Save**.
- **Edit / Delete:** Use the action icons next to each designation.

## 5. Employee Management (HRM Fields)

When the HRM module is active, extra fields are added to every user (employee) profile. When you create or edit a user under **User Management**, you'll see additional HRM fields:

| Field | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| **Department** | Which department the employee belongs to. |
| **Designation** | Their job title. |
| **Sub-Department** | A more specific team within the department (optional). |
| **Location** | Which business location they work at. |

| **Appointment Date** | The date they started working. |

### How to Set These Up

1. Go to **User Management** → click **Edit** on an employee.
2. Scroll down to the **HRM** section.
3. Fill in Department, Designation, Salary, etc.
4. Click **Update**.

## 6. Shifts

**What is it?** Shifts define the working hours for your employees (e.g., Morning 9 AM – 5 PM, Night 10 PM – 6 AM).

**How to get here:** HRM → **Attendance** tab → click on the **Shifts** section, or go to Shift management.

### 6.1 Types of Shifts

| Shift Type | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Fixed Shift** | Has a set start and end time (e.g., 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM). |
| **Flexible Shift** | Employees can clock in within a time range — no strict start/end. |

### 6.2 Creating a Shift

1. Click **+ Add Shift**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Shift Name** (e.g., "Morning Shift")
   - **Shift Type** (Fixed or Flexible)
   - **Start Time** and **End Time**
   - **Working Days** — select which days of the week this shift runs (e.g., Monday–Friday).
   - **Holidays** — select holidays when this shift is off.
   - **Auto Clock-Out** — if turned on, the system will automatically clock out employees at the end time.
   - **Count Overtime** — whether extra hours beyond the shift end should count as overtime.
3. Click **Save**.

### 6.3 Assigning Employees to Shifts

1. On the shift you created, click **Assign Users**.
2. Select the employees you want to assign.
3. Set the **Start Date** and **End Date** for this assignment (e.g., the employee works this shift from Jan 1 to Dec 31).
4. Click **Save**.

> **Important:** An employee must be assigned to a shift before they can clock in. The system checks shift assignments to validate attendance.

## 7. Attendance

**What is it?** Track when employees start and finish work each day.

**How to get here:** HRM → **Attendance** tab.

### 7.1 Clock In / Clock Out

**For Employees:**
- A **Clock In** button appears in the header/top bar of the system.
- Click it to record your arrival time.
- When you're leaving, click **Clock Out**.
- You can optionally add a **note** (e.g., "Working from home today").
- If location tracking is enabled in settings, your GPS location will be recorded.

**Rules:**
- You can only clock in if you have an active shift assigned for today.
- Grace periods (set in settings) allow clocking in a few minutes before/after the shift starts.
- If the shift has **Auto Clock-Out** enabled, you'll be automatically clocked out at shift end.

### 7.2 Managing Attendance (Admin/HR)

As an admin or HR manager, you can:

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| **View All Attendance** | Go to HRM → Attendance. You'll see a table of all records with date, employee name, clock in/out times, and duration. |
| **Add Attendance Manually** | Click **+ Add**, select the employee, enter clock-in and clock-out times, and save. Useful when someone forgot to clock in. |
| **Edit Attendance** | Click **Edit** on a record to change the times. |
| **Delete Attendance** | Click **Delete** to remove a record. |
| **Filter by Shift** | Use the shift filter dropdown to see attendance for a specific shift. |
| **Filter by Date** | Use the date picker to view a specific day's attendance. |
| **Delete Duplicates** | Click **Delete Duplicates** to automatically clean up duplicate records. |

### 7.3 Import Attendance

If you have attendance data from an external source (a file or biometric terminal):

**From File:**
1. Click **Import Attendance**.
2. Upload a CSV/Excel file with columns: Employee ID, Clock In, Clock Out.
3. Click **Import**.

**From External Terminal:**
1. Go to HRM → Attendance → **Sync Attendance**.
2. Make sure you've configured the terminal API Key and Secret in Settings.
3. Click **Sync** to pull records from the terminal.

### 7.4 Monthly Attendance Report

1. Go to HRM → Attendance → **Monthly Report**.
2. Select the **Month** and **Year**.
3. You'll see a grid showing each employee's attendance for every day of the month.
4. The report shows total days worked, overtime hours, and any gaps.

### 7.5 Attendance Summary (Per Employee)

- Click on an employee's name in the attendance list.
- You'll see their personal summary: total hours worked, overtime, and late arrivals for the selected period.

## 8. Leave Management

**What is it?** Manage employee time-off requests — sick leave, vacation, personal days, etc.

### 8.1 Leave Types

**How to get here:** HRM → **Leave Type** tab.

Before employees can apply for leave, you need to set up leave types.

**Creating a Leave Type:**
1. Click **+ Add**.
2. Enter:
   - **Leave Type Name** (e.g., "Sick Leave", "Annual Leave", "Maternity Leave").
   - **Maximum Leave Count** — how many days an employee can take per interval (e.g., 12).
   - **Leave Count Interval** — "Per Month" or "Per Year".
3. Click **Save**.

**Examples:**

| Leave Type | Max Days | Interval |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Sick Leave | 10 | Per Year |
| Annual Leave | 15 | Per Year |
| Casual Leave | 2 | Per Month |

### 8.2 Applying for Leave (Employee)

1. Go to HRM → **Leave** tab.
2. Click **+ Add Leave**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Leave Type** — select from the dropdown.
   - **Start Date** and **End Date**.
   - **Reason** — explain why you need the time off.
4. Click **Submit**.

Your leave request will be set to **Pending** status and your manager/admin will be notified.

### 8.3 Approving / Rejecting Leave (Manager/Admin)

1. Go to HRM → **Leave** tab.
2. You'll see all leave requests. Pending ones are highlighted.
3. Click on the **Status** button to open the approval modal.
4. Choose:
   - **Approved** — the leave is granted.
   - **Cancelled** — the leave is rejected.
5. Add an optional **Status Note** (e.g., "Approved, enjoy your vacation!").
6. Click **Save**.

The employee will receive a notification about the status change.

### 8.4 Leave Activity Log

- Click on a leave request to see its full history — when it was created, who changed the status, and any notes.

### 8.5 Leave Summary

- Click **User Leave Summary** to see a per-employee breakdown of how many days they've taken vs. how many remain for each leave type.

## 9. Leave Quotas

**What is it?** Leave quotas let you assign a specific number of leave days to each employee for a fiscal year, and track how many they've used.

**How to get here:** HRM → More ▼ → **Leave Quotas**.

### Setting Up Leave Quotas

1. Click **+ Add Quota**.
2. Select the **Employee**.
3. Select the **Leave Type** (e.g., Annual Leave).
4. Enter the **Fiscal Year** (e.g., 2026).
5. Enter **Allocated Days** (e.g., 15).
6. Optionally enter **Carried Forward** days from the previous year.
7. Click **Save**.

The system will automatically track:
- **Used Days** — updated when approved leave is recorded.
- **Remaining Days** — calculated as (Allocated + Carried Forward − Used).

> **Tip:** When an employee applies for leave, the system can check their quota to ensure they have enough days available.

## 11. Payroll

**What is it?** Create payslips for employees, calculate total pay including allowances and deductions, and process payments.

**How to get here:** HRM → **Payroll** tab.

### 11.1 Creating a Payroll (Individual)

1. Click **+ Add Payroll**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Employee** — select from the dropdown.
   - **Month/Year** — select the pay period (e.g., January 2026).
   - **Duration** and **Duration Unit** — (e.g., 30 days, or 1 month).
   - **Amount Per Unit Duration** — the base salary for this period.
3. **Allowances** will be automatically listed based on what's assigned to the employee.
   - You can add/edit individual allowance amounts.
4. **Deductions** will also be auto-populated.
   - You can add/edit individual deduction amounts.
5. The **Gross Amount** is calculated automatically:
   > Gross = Base Salary + Total Allowances − Total Deductions
6. Click **Save**.

### 11.2 Viewing a Payslip

- Click on a payroll record to see the full payslip.
- The payslip shows:
  - Employee name, department, designation
  - Pay period
  - Base salary
  - Each allowance with amount
  - Each deduction with amount
  - Net pay (gross amount)

### 11.3 Payroll Groups (Batch Payroll)

Instead of creating payroll one employee at a time, you can process payroll for a group:

1. Go to HRM → Payroll → you'll see a **Payroll Groups** section.
2. View existing groups or create a new one.
3. A payroll group contains multiple individual payroll records for a department or location.
4. You can:
   - **Filter by Location** or **Filter by Department** to select employees.
   - **Add Payment** to the group — record actual payment (cash, bank transfer, etc.).
   - **View Payments** — see all payments made for this group.
   - **Edit/Delete Payments** as needed.

### 11.4 Advance Payments

Sometimes employees request salary advances:

1. Go to HRM → Payroll → **Advance Payments**.
2. Click **+ Add Advance Payment**.
3. Select the employee, enter the amount, and save.
4. Advance payments can be deducted from future payroll.

### 11.5 My Payrolls (Employee View)

Regular employees can view their own payroll history:
1. Go to HRM → Payroll → **My Payrolls**.
2. See a list of all your payslips with dates and amounts.

### 11.6 Monthly Payroll Report

1. Go to HRM → Payroll → **Monthly Report**.
2. Select the month/year.
3. View a summary of all payroll processed for that month, totals by department, and payment statuses.

## 12. Allowances & Deductions

**What is it?** Define the extra components that get added to or subtracted from an employee's base salary.

**How to get here:** HRM → Settings OR Essentials → from the Allowances & Deductions section.

### Types

| Type | Examples |
|------|---------|
| **Allowance** (addition) | Housing Allowance, Transport Allowance, Meal Allowance, Overtime Pay |
| **Deduction** (subtraction) | Tax, Social Security, Loan Repayment, Late Penalty |

### Creating an Allowance/Deduction

1. Click **+ Add**.
2. Enter:
   - **Description** (e.g., "Housing Allowance").
   - **Type** — Allowance or Deduction.
   - **Amount** — the value.
   - **Amount Type:**
     - **Fixed** — a flat amount (e.g., $500).
     - **Percentage** — a percentage of the base salary (e.g., 10%).
   - **Applicable Date** — when this component starts applying (optional).
3. Click **Save**.

### Assigning to Employees

Allowances and deductions can be assigned to employees in two ways:
1. **From the employee profile:** When editing a user, scroll to the HRM section and select the applicable pay components.
2. **Automatically in payroll:** When creating a payroll, the system loads all components assigned to that employee.

## 13. Sales Targets & Commissions

**What is it?** Set sales targets for employees and define commission percentages based on their performance.

**How to get here:** HRM → **Sales Target** tab.

### Setting a Sales Target

1. You'll see a list of employees.
2. Click **Set Target** next to an employee.
3. Enter:
   - **Target Start Date** and **Target End Date** (the period the target covers).
   - **Commission Percentage** — the % the employee earns on sales achieved.
4. Click **Save**.

### How It Works

- The system tracks each employee's sales during the target period.
- Commission is calculated based on the total sales amount × commission percentage.
- There's a setting (in HRM Settings → Sales Target tab) to choose whether commission is calculated **with or without tax**.

### Viewing Sales Targets

- The Essentials Dashboard has a **User Sales Targets** widget that shows each user's target vs. actual performance.

## 15. Warnings & Discipline

**What is it?** Issue formal warnings to employees and track disciplinary actions.

**How to get here:** HRM → More ▼ → **Warnings**.

### Issuing a Warning

1. Click **+ Add Warning**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Employee** — who is receiving the warning.

     - **Verbal** — a spoken warning, documented for records.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|

| **Acknowledged** | The employee has acknowledged receiving the warning. |

### Managing Warnings

- **Update Status:** Click the status button on a warning to change it (e.g., from Active to Acknowledged).
- **Employee Response:** Employees can submit a response through the system.
- **View Details:** Click a warning to see the full details and history.

## 28. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General

**Q: I can't see the HRM or Essentials menu in the sidebar.**
A: Your user account may not have the required permissions. Ask your administrator to grant you "Access HRM" and/or "Access Essentials" permissions. Also, verify the module is activated in the subscription.

**Q: Can I use HRM & Essentials on multiple business locations?**
A: Yes. Most features (holidays, announcements, attendance) can be scoped to specific locations. Payroll groups can also be filtered by location.

### Attendance

**Q: An employee forgot to clock in. What should I do?**
A: An administrator can manually add an attendance record: HRM → Attendance → + Add. Enter the employee, clock-in time, and clock-out time.

**Q: What happens if an employee clocks in too early or too late?**
A: The system checks against the shift's grace period settings. If the employee tries to clock in outside the allowed window, the system will show an error. Adjust the grace period in Settings if needed.

**Q: How does overtime calculation work?**
A: When an employee works beyond their shift end time, the extra time is calculated as overtime. It's multiplied by the **Overtime Multiplier** set in Settings (e.g., 1.5×). The overtime can be rounded to the nearest hour based on the "Round To" setting.

**Q: We use a biometric terminal. How do I connect it?**
A: Go to HRM → Settings → Attendance tab. Enter your terminal's **API Key** and **API Secret**. Each employee needs an **Attendance Terminal UID** set in their profile. Then use the **Sync Attendance** button to pull records.

### Leave

**Q: Can I set different leave limits for different employees?**
A: Yes! Use **Leave Quotas** (HRM → More → Leave Quotas). You can assign different numbers of days to individual employees for each leave type and fiscal year. You can also carry forward unused days.

**Q: How do I see how many leave days an employee has left?**
A: Go to HRM → Leave → click **User Leave Summary**, or check their Leave Quota record.

### Payroll

**Q: How is the gross pay calculated?**
A: Gross Pay = Base Salary + All Allowances − All Deductions. The system automatically pulls in the allowances and deductions assigned to the employee.

**Q: Can I process payroll for an entire department at once?**
A: Yes, use **Payroll Groups**. Filter by location or department, and process all salaries together.

**Q: How do advance payments work?**
A: Add an advance payment from HRM → Payroll → Advance Payments. The amount is recorded and can be factored into subsequent payroll as a deduction.

### Tasks

**Q: Can I assign a task to multiple people?**
A: Yes. When creating or editing a task, you can select multiple employees in the "Assign To" field.

**Q: Can I attach files to tasks?**
A: Yes. Inside a task's detail view, use the **Upload Document** button to attach files.
