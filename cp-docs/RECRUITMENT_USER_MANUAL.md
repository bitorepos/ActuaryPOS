## 1. Introduction

### What Is the Recruitment Module?

The **Recruitment Module** adds a complete hiring management system to your POS/business software. It helps you manage the entire hiring process from start to finish:

1. **Post job openings** — define what positions you need to fill.
2. **Collect candidates** — add candidates manually or let them apply through a public portal.
3. **Track applications** — move candidates through a 10-stage pipeline.
4. **Schedule interviews** — organize and manage interviews.
5. **Evaluate candidates** — score candidates using customizable evaluation forms.
6. **Send offers** — create and track job offers.
7. **Onboard new hires** — manage the onboarding checklist for hired candidates.

### Who Should Read This Manual?

- **Business owners** who need to hire new employees.
- **HR managers/recruiters** who handle the hiring process.
- **Hiring managers** who participate in interviews and approvals.
- **Administrators** who set up the system and manage permissions.

## 4. Job Openings

**What is it?** A job opening represents a position you want to fill. It's the starting point of the recruitment process.

**How to get here:** Recruitment → **Job Openings** tab.

### 4.1 Creating a Job Opening

1. Click **+ Add Job Opening**.
2. Fill in the details:

| Field | What to Enter | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Title** | The job title. | "Sales Associate" |
| **Reference Number** | An optional internal reference code. | "JOB-2026-001" |
| **Business Location** | Which location needs this hire. | "Main Store" |
| **Department** | Which department. | "Sales" |
| **Designation** | The job designation/level. | "Junior Associate" |
| **Employment Type** | The type of employment. | Full-time / Part-time / Contract / Internship / Temporary |
| **Number of Positions** | How many people you need to hire. | 2 |
| **Description** | Detailed description of the job role and responsibilities. | "Responsible for customer service, product display..." |
| **Requirements** | What qualifications and skills are needed. | "Must have 1 year retail experience..." |
| **Benefits** | What you offer to candidates. | "Health insurance, meal allowance, staff discount..." |
| **Salary Range** | Minimum and maximum salary. | Min: $2,000 / Max: $3,000 |
| **Salary Period** | How salary is paid. | Monthly / Yearly / Hourly / Weekly |
| **Opening Date** | When the position opens for applications. | 2026-03-01 |
| **Closing Date** | When applications close. | 2026-04-30 |
| **Hiring Manager** | The person responsible for this hire. | "John Smith" |
| **Is Published** | Whether this job shows on the public portal. | Yes / No |

3. Click **Save**.

| Status | Meaning |

| **Open** | Actively accepting applications. |
| **On Hold** | Temporarily paused (not accepting new applications). |

| **Cancelled** | The job opening has been cancelled entirely. |

### 4.3 Managing Job Openings

## 6. Applications & Pipeline

**What is it?** An application connects a candidate to a specific job opening and tracks their progress through the hiring process.

**How to get here:** Recruitment → **Applications** tab.

### 6.1 Creating an Application

1. Click **+ Add Application**.
2. Select:
   - **Job Opening** — which position is this application for.
   - **Candidate** — which candidate is applying.
   - **Applied Date** — when the application was submitted.
   - **Assigned To** — which recruiter/HR person is responsible for this application.
3. Click **Save**.

> **Note:** Each candidate can only apply once per job opening. The system prevents duplicate applications.

### 6.2 The 10-Stage Pipeline

Every application moves through these stages:

| Stage | What It Means | What to Do |
|-------|-------------|-----------|
| 1. **Applied** | The candidate has submitted their application. | Review their resume and qualifications. |
| 2. **Screening** | Initial review of the candidate's background. | Check if they meet minimum requirements. |
| 3. **Shortlisted** | The candidate meets the requirements and is selected for further evaluation. | Prepare for interview scheduling. |
| 4. **Interview Scheduled** | An interview has been arranged. | Conduct the interview on the scheduled date. |
| 5. **Interviewed** | The interview has been completed. | Collect feedback from interviewers. |
| 6. **Evaluation** | The candidate is being formally evaluated/scored. | Complete evaluation forms. |
| 7. **Offered** | A job offer has been sent. | Wait for the candidate's response. |
| 8. **Hired** | The candidate has accepted and is hired! 🎉 | Start the onboarding process. |
| 9. **Rejected** | The candidate was not selected. | Send a rejection notification (optional). |
| 10. **Withdrawn** | The candidate withdrew their application. | Close the application. |

### 6.3 Moving a Candidate Through the Pipeline

1. Open the application.
2. Click **Update Stage**.
3. Select the new stage from the dropdown.
4. Optionally add **Stage Notes** (e.g., "Passed phone screening, proceeding to interview").
5. You can also set a **Rating** (1–5 stars) for the candidate at this stage.
6. Click **Save**.

> **Important:** Some stage changes happen automatically:
> - Creating an **Interview** automatically moves the application to "Interview Scheduled".
> - Creating an **Offer** automatically moves the application to "Offered".

### 6.4 Application List Features

- **Filter by Job Opening** — see applications for a specific position.
- **Filter by Stage** — see all candidates at a particular stage (e.g., all "Shortlisted" candidates).
- **Filter by Assigned Recruiter** — see your own workload.
- **Sort by Date or Rating** — prioritize candidates.

## 7. Interview Scheduling

**What is it?** Schedule, track, and evaluate interviews for candidates.

**How to get here:** Recruitment → **Interviews** tab.

### 7.1 Scheduling an Interview

1. Click **+ Add Interview**.
2. Fill in:

| Field | What to Enter | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Application** | Select the application (shows candidate name + job title). | "Jane Doe – Sales Associate" |
| **Interview Type** | The format of the interview. | In Person / Phone / Video / Panel / Technical / HR |

| **End Time** | When the interview ends. | 10:45 AM |

| **Meeting Link** | URL for video calls (for virtual interviews). | "https://zoom.us/j/123456" |
| **Interviewers** | Select one or more interviewers from your staff. | "John Smith, Mary Johnson" |
| **Notes** | Any special instructions for the interview. | "Please prepare technical questions on inventory management." |

3. Click **Save**.

> When you create an interview, the application's stage is automatically updated to **"Interview Scheduled"**.

### 7.2 Interview Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Scheduled** | The interview is planned and upcoming. |
| **In Progress** | The interview is currently happening. |
| **Completed** | The interview has finished. |
| **Cancelled** | The interview was cancelled. |
| **Rescheduled** | The interview has been moved to a new date/time. |
| **No Show** | The candidate did not attend. |

### 7.3 After the Interview — Recording Feedback

Once an interview is completed:

1. Open the interview record.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Status** → change to "Completed".
   - **Overall Rating** — rate the candidate from 1 to 5.
   - **Feedback** — write a detailed summary of how the interview went.
   - **Recommendation:**
     - **Strong Hire** — highly recommend hiring this person.
     - **Hire** — recommend hiring.
     - **No Hire** — do not recommend hiring.
     - **Strong No Hire** — strongly advise against hiring.
     - **Pending** — not yet decided.
4. Click **Update**.

### 7.4 Calendar View

You can view all interviews on a calendar:
- Go to **Interviews** → switch to **Calendar View**.
- Interviews are displayed on their scheduled dates.
- Click on an interview event to see the details.

## 8. Candidate Evaluation

**What is it?** A structured scoring system to objectively evaluate candidates based on predefined criteria.

> **Note:** This is available through the advanced settings. Set up evaluation criteria and forms before using this feature.

### 8.1 Setting Up Evaluation Criteria

**How to get here:** Recruitment → Settings → **Evaluation Criteria** tab.

1. Click **+ Add Criteria**.
2. Enter:
   - **Criteria Title** (e.g., "Communication Skills", "Technical Knowledge", "Teamwork").
   - **Criteria Type** — either "Group" (a category) or "Child" (a specific item under a group).
   - **Group** — if this is a child criteria, select its parent group.
   - **Description** — what this criteria measures.
   - **Score Descriptions** — define what each score means:
     - Score 1: "Very Poor"
     - Score 2: "Below Average"
     - Score 3: "Average"
     - Score 4: "Good"
     - Score 5: "Excellent"
3. Click **Save**.

**Example Criteria Setup:**

| Group | Criteria | What It Measures |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Technical Skills | Programming | Coding ability |
| Technical Skills | Problem Solving | Analytical thinking |
| Soft Skills | Communication | Verbal and written communication |
| Soft Skills | Teamwork | Ability to work in a team |
| Culture Fit | Company Values | Alignment with company values |

### 8.2 Creating Evaluation Forms

**How to get here:** Recruitment → Settings → **Evaluation Form** tab.

1. Click **+ Add Form**.
2. Enter:
   - **Form Name** (e.g., "Sales Associate Evaluation").
   - **Position** — which job position this form is for.
3. Add criteria groups with **weight percentages**:
   - Technical Skills: 40%
   - Soft Skills: 35%
   - Culture Fit: 25%
   - (Total must equal 100%)
4. Click **Save**.

### 8.3 Evaluating a Candidate

1. Open a candidate's detail page.
2. Go to the **Evaluation** section.
3. Select the evaluation form.
4. Score each criteria from 1 to 5.
5. Add your feedback comments.
6. Click **Submit Evaluation**.

The system calculates the weighted average score automatically.

## 10. Onboarding

**What is it?** Once a candidate accepts an offer and is hired, the onboarding process helps you get them set up and ready to work.

**How to get here:** Recruitment → **Onboarding** tab.

### 10.1 Creating an Onboarding Record

1. Click **+ Add Onboarding**.
2. Fill in:

| Field | What to Enter |
|-------|-------------|
| **Application** | Select the hired application. |
| **Candidate** | Auto-filled based on the application. |
| **Joining Date** | The new hire's first day. |
| **Assigned Buddy** | A current employee who will help the new hire settle in. |
| **Status** | Pending / In Progress / Completed / Cancelled. |

3. Click **Save**.

### 10.2 Onboarding Checklist

Each onboarding record has a built-in checklist:

| Checklist Item | What It Means |
|---------------|--------------|
| ☐ **Document Verification** | Have all required documents (ID, certificates, etc.) been verified? |
| ☐ **Equipment Assigned** | Has the new hire been given their laptop, phone, keys, uniform, etc.? |
| ☐ **Access Provided** | Have system logins, email accounts, and access cards been created? |
| ☐ **Training Scheduled** | Has initial training been arranged? |
| ☐ **Policy Acknowledged** | Has the new hire read and signed company policies? |

Check each item as it's completed. The system calculates a **Completion Percentage** (0% to 100%) automatically.

### 10.3 Onboarding Notes

Use the **Checklist Notes** field to add any additional information about the onboarding process (e.g., "Waiting for IT to set up workstation").

### 10.4 Onboarding Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Onboarding hasn't started yet. |
| **In Progress** | The new hire is going through onboarding steps. |
| **Completed** | All checklist items are done — the employee is fully onboarded! |
| **Cancelled** | Onboarding was cancelled (e.g., the hire fell through). |

## 11. Recruitment Plans (Proposals)

**What is it?** A recruitment plan is a formal request to hire for specific positions. It includes details about what you need, why you need it, and who needs to approve it.

> **Note:** This feature may be hidden by default. To enable it, go to Settings → Recruitment Campaign Settings and set "Show recruitment plan" to the appropriate option.

**How to get here:** Recruitment → **Plans** tab.

### 11.1 Creating a Recruitment Plan

1. Click **+ Add Plan**.
2. Fill in:

| Field | What to Enter | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Plan Name** | A descriptive name. | "Q2 2026 Sales Hiring" |
| **Position** | The job position to fill. | "Sales Associate" |
| **Department** | Which department needs the hire. | "Sales" |
| **Number of Recruits** | How many people to hire. | 3 |
| **Form of Work** | Work arrangement. | Full-time / Part-time / Contract |
| **Workplace** | Where they'll work. | "Main Branch" |
| **Starting Salary** / **To Salary** | Salary range. | $2,000 – $3,000 |

4. Click **Save**.

### 11.2 Plan Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|

| **In Progress** | Actively recruiting. |

## 13. Recruitment Channels & Web Forms

**What is it?** A recruitment channel is a custom web form that you can embed on your website or share as a link. When someone fills it out, their information is automatically added to your candidate database.

**How to get here:** Recruitment → **Channels** tab.

### 13.1 Creating a Recruitment Channel

1. Click **+ Add Channel**.
2. You'll see a **drag-and-drop form builder**.
3. Build your form by adding fields:
   - **Text fields** — name, email, phone.
   - **Dropdown selects** — experience level, position applied for.
   - **Checkboxes** — agree to terms.
   - **Date pickers** — availability date.
   - **File upload** — resume/CV upload.
   - **Text areas** — cover letter or introduction.
4. Configure:
   - **reCAPTCHA** — enable to prevent spam submissions.
   - **Notification email** — receive an alert when someone applies.
   - **Linked Campaign** — associate with a specific recruitment campaign.
5. Click **Save**.

### 13.2 Sharing the Form

After creating a channel, you'll get:
- A **direct link** you can share via email, social media, or job boards.
- An **embed code** to place the form on your website.

### 13.3 How It Works

1. A job seeker visits the form link or your website.
2. They fill in their details and upload their resume.
3. The submission is automatically saved as a new candidate in your database.
4. You receive a notification.
5. You can then create an application for that candidate and start the pipeline process.

## 14. Recruitment Portal (Public Job Board)

**What is it?** A public-facing website page where job seekers can browse your open positions, view job details, and apply.

**How to get here:** Recruitment → **Portal** tab (admin view), or visit the portal URL directly.

### 14.1 What Job Seekers See

The portal displays:
- **Job Listings** — all published, open job openings.
- **Search & Filter** — search by keyword, filter by location, department, or employment type.
- **Job Details** — click on a job to see full description, requirements, benefits, and salary.
- **"Apply Now" Button** — links to the application form (recruitment channel form).
- **"Email to Friend"** — share a job listing with someone via email.
- **Responsive Design** — works on mobile phones and tablets.

### 14.2 Setting Up the Portal (Admin)

- Jobs appear on the portal when they are marked as **"Published"** and have **"Open"** status.
- To hide a job from the portal, uncheck the **"Is Published"** checkbox on the job opening.
- The portal supports **multiple languages** (24 languages available) and **RTL (right-to-left)** languages.

### 14.3 Portal URL

The portal is accessible at:
```
https://yourdomain.com/recruitment/recruitment_portal
```

You can link to this from your main website, social media, or email signatures.

## 15. Kanban Board View

**What is it?** A visual board (like a Trello board) that shows candidates organized by their status in columns. Great for getting a quick overview and managing candidates with drag-and-drop.

**How to get here:** Recruitment → Candidates → switch to **Kanban View**.

### How the Kanban Board Works

The board has columns for each candidate status:

| Column | Color |
|--------|-------|
| Application | Blue |
| Potential | Light Blue |
| Interview | Purple |
| Won Interview | Green |
| Send Offer | Orange |
| Elect | Dark Green |
| Non-Elect | Red |
| Unanswered | Gray |
| Transferred | Teal |
| Freedom | Light Gray |

Each candidate appears as a **card** showing:
- Name and photo
- Position applied for
- Current rating (stars)
- Campaign name

### Actions on the Kanban Board

- **Drag a card** from one column to another to change the candidate's status.
- **Click a card** to open the candidate's full profile.
- **Load more** — if there are many candidates, click "Load More" at the bottom of a column.

### Setting Up Transfer Steps

1. Click **+ Add Step**.

| Field | What to Enter | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Order** | Step number (1, 2, 3...). | 1 |
| **Send To** | Who receives the notification. | Candidate / Specific Staff Member / Department Head |
| **Email To** | The email address or variable. | "{candidate_email}" or "hr@company.com" |
| **Subject** | Email subject line. | "Welcome to Company — Document Submission Required" |
| **Content** | Email body (supports rich text and variables). | "Dear {candidate_name}, please submit the following documents..." |
| **Attachment** | Upload any files to include. | "Document_Checklist.pdf" |

3. Click **Save**.

### How It Works

When you click **"Transfer to HR"** on a hired candidate:
1. The system sends Step 1's email.
2. Once acknowledged, it moves to Step 2.
3. This continues through all steps.
4. At the final step, the candidate can be automatically created as an employee in the HR module.

## 21. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Getting Started

**Q: I can't see the Recruitment option in the sidebar.**
A: Your user account may not have the "Access Recruitment Module" permission. Ask your administrator to grant it. Also make sure the module is activated in your subscription plan.

**Q: How do I start hiring for a new position?**
A: Follow this process:
1. Create a **Job Opening** with all the details.
2. Publish it so it appears on the portal (optional).
3. As candidates come in, add them to the **Candidates** database.
4. Create **Applications** to link candidates to the job opening.
5. Move candidates through the pipeline stages as they progress.
6. Schedule **Interviews**.
7. Record interview feedback.
8. Send **Offers** to the top candidates.
9. Start **Onboarding** when they accept.

### Job Openings

**Q: Can I post the same job at multiple locations?**
A: Create a separate job opening for each location, or leave the location field empty to indicate it's company-wide.

**Q: What happens when I close a job opening?**
A: The job is removed from the public portal and no new applications can be submitted. Existing applications remain in the system.

### Candidates

**Q: A candidate applied for two different positions. How do I handle this?**
A: Add the candidate once to the database. Then create two separate **Applications** — one for each job opening. Each application tracks independently through the pipeline.

**Q: Can candidates apply on their own?**
A: Yes! Set up a **Recruitment Channel** (web form) and share the link, or enable the **Recruitment Portal** where candidates can browse jobs and apply.

### Interviews

**Q: How do I avoid scheduling conflicts?**
A: The system checks for time conflicts. Use the **Calendar View** to visually check interviewer availability before scheduling.

**Q: Can I schedule multiple rounds of interviews?**
A: Yes. Create a separate interview record for each round (e.g., "Phone Screen", "Technical Interview", "HR Interview") for the same application.

### Offers

**Q: What if a candidate wants to negotiate?**
A: Change the offer status to **"Negotiating"**. Edit the offer to adjust the salary or terms. Update the **Candidate Remarks** field with their counter-offer.

**Q: The offer expired. What should I do?**
A: You can either edit the offer to extend the "Valid Until" date or create a new offer.

### Onboarding

**Q: How do I know what percentage of onboarding is complete?**
A: The system automatically calculates a **Completion Percentage** based on how many of the 5 checklist items are checked off. 3 out of 5 = 60%.

**Q: Can I add custom onboarding steps?**
A: The built-in checklist has 5 items. Use the **Checklist Notes** field for additional items. For more advanced workflows, use the **Transfer Personnel** settings to create a multi-step email-based onboarding process.

### Recruitment Portal

**Q: How do I make the portal look professional?**
A: The portal uses a pre-built responsive template. Ensure your job descriptions are well-written, include salary ranges when appropriate, and highlight benefits. Your company logo is automatically included.

**Q: Can the portal work in other languages?**
A: Yes! The module supports 24 languages. The portal automatically adapts to the language setting, including right-to-left (RTL) language support.

### General

**Q: Can I see recruitment data on the dashboard?**
A: Yes, the Recruitment Dashboard shows all key metrics — open jobs, candidate pipeline, upcoming interviews, and pending offers.

**Q: Is there a way to export candidate data?**
A: You can export individual candidate profiles as **PDF documents** using the Export/Print function on their profile page.

**Q: How does the Recruitment module connect to the HRM module?**
A: When a candidate is hired and transferred to HR (using the Transfer to HR feature), they can be automatically created as an employee in the HRM/Essentials module, with their details pre-filled. This creates a seamless transition from hiring to employee management.
