## Dashboard Sections Explained

### Sales Summary

At the top of your Dashboard, you'll see your sales totals:

| Card | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Total Sales** | The total value of all sales in the selected time period |
| **Total Purchase** | The total value of all purchases |
| **Invoice Due** | Money your customers still owe you |
| **Purchase Due** | Money you still owe your suppliers |

📸 *[Screenshot: The four summary cards at the top of the Dashboard]*

> 💡 **Tip:** Click on any card to jump straight to the detailed report for that category.

---

### Sales & Purchase Chart

Below the summary cards, you'll see a visual chart showing:

- **Sales trend** over time (blue line or bars)
- **Purchase trend** over time (red line or bars)

You can change the time range using the date picker.

📸 *[Screenshot: The sales and purchase trend chart]*

### Stock Alerts

This section shows products that are **running low**. When a product's quantity falls below its reorder level, it appears here as a warning.

- **Product Name** — The item that's running low
- **Current Stock** — How many you have left
- **Reorder Level** — The minimum you set for this product

📸 *[Screenshot: The Stock Alerts section with low-stock items listed]*

> ⚠️ **Important:** Keep an eye on this section daily so you never run out of your best-selling items.

---

### Payment Due (Receivable)

This section lists customers who owe you money:

- **Customer Name** — Who owes you
- **Invoice Number** — Which sale is unpaid
- **Amount Due** — How much they owe
- **Due Date** — When the payment is expected

📸 *[Screenshot: The payment due table showing outstanding customer invoices]*

---

### Payment Due (Payable)

This section shows your outstanding payments to suppliers:

- **Supplier Name** — Who you owe money to
- **Purchase Reference** — Which purchase is unpaid
- **Amount Due** — How much you owe
- **Due Date** — When you need to pay

## How to Change the Dashboard Date Range

1. Look at the top of the Dashboard for the **date range selector**.
2. Click on it and choose a preset range:
   - **Today** — See just today's numbers
   - **This Week** — Current week summary
   - **This Month** — Current month
   - **This Financial Year** — Full year overview
   - **Custom Range** — Pick your own start and end dates
3. The Dashboard numbers will refresh automatically.

## How to Customise Your Dashboard

Your administrator can control what appears on your Dashboard:

1. Go to **Settings → Business Settings**.
2. Click the **Dashboard** tab.
3. Choose which sections to show or hide.
4. Set the default date range for the Dashboard.
5. Click **Update Settings**.

## Common Questions

**Q: Why are my numbers showing zero?**
A: Check the date range at the top — you might be looking at a period with no transactions. Also make sure you've recorded at least one sale or purchase.

**Q: Can different users see different Dashboards?**
A: The Dashboard shows data based on your access permissions. If you can only see your own sales, you'll only see your numbers. Administrators see everything.

**Q: How often does the Dashboard update?**
A: The numbers update every time you load the page. For real-time updates, refresh your browser.

## Tips & Best Practices

- 📌 Check your Dashboard first thing every morning to stay on top of your business
- 📌 Review the **Stock Alerts** section daily to avoid stockouts
- 📌 Follow up on overdue payments shown in the **Payment Due** sections
- 📌 Use the **This Month** view at month-end for a quick performance check
