# Resetting Data, Deleting All Data Or Transactions

Use this guide when you need to delete individual records or start fresh with a clean business account in {application_name}.

> **Warning:** Deleted transactions and records may not be recoverable. Always take a backup before deleting important data.

---

## Deleting Products, Sales And Purchase Transactions

To delete individual records such as products, sales, purchases, or other transactions:

1. Go to the relevant list page, for example:
   - **Products > List Products**
   - **Sell > All Sales**
   - **Purchases > List Purchases**
2. Find the entry you want to remove.
3. Open the **Actions** menu for that entry.
4. Choose **Delete**.
5. Confirm the deletion only after checking the correct record is selected.

Deleted records can affect reports, stock, payments, and account balances. Review the impact before deleting live business data.

---

## Resetting All Data

If you want a fresh start, the recommended approach is:

1. Keep the current business as a test or dummy business.
2. Register or create a new business for actual use.
3. Configure the new business settings.
4. Start entering live data in the new business.

Creating a new business gives you a clean environment with no previous transactions, while keeping the old business available for testing or reference.

---

## Before Deleting Or Resetting

Before making major data changes:

- Export or back up the database.
- Export important reports.
- Confirm users are not actively entering transactions.
- Confirm which business/location is being changed.
- Ask the administrator if you are unsure.
